# 生成提示词

工具：Codex 内置 imagegen。三次调用均设置 `transparent_background=true`，使用参考图片路径传入素材。

## 形象图

输入：用户提供的两张科小牛参考图片。

```text
Create ONE polished isolated full-body USTC 科小牛 research assistant mascot, 2048x2048 square transparent PNG. Reference image 1 is the PRIMARY exact character identity/style reference: use ONLY its small full-body cream/beige ox mascot, ignore lanyard, card, seal and all surrounding objects. Reference image 2 supports identity only; do not use its red background or exaggerated expression. Preserve original very large rounded oblong beige head, tiny brown upward horns, little tousled hair tuft, side ears, very large white rounded muzzle, tiny nose circles, rosy cheeks, short pear-shaped body, small brown hooves. Clean simple FLAT 2D cartoon with warm tan outlines, opaque pastel color fills, minimal shading, friendly calm wide-open black oval eyes with white highlights and small curved smile. Straight front view, upright full-body standing, BOTH arms naturally down at sides, both hooves visible, centered with comfortable margin. Add perfectly ROUND BLACK THICK-RIMMED EYEGLASSES with thin connecting bridge, visibly transparent lens interiors and eyes clearly visible. White lab coat is BUTTONED CLOSED, not open: small fold-over lapels, centered vertical row of buttons, TWO EMPTY LOWER PATCH POCKETS, hem just above feet; tiny blue inner collar visible. Exact blue letters 'USTC' on CHARACTER'S LEFT CHEST (viewer right). No tools, accessories, clipboard, badge, tie, robot decorations, props, additional text, scenery or ground shadow. Maintain the unmistakable original 科小牛 face and proportions. True transparent background with alpha, not a checkerboard drawing. This finished image will be used as identity reference for separate editable component layers.
```

Remove ONLY these separate overlay features: BOTH eyeglasses and bridge completely, BOTH black eyes including their white eye outlines and BOTH eyebrows completely, the small curved mouth smile completely, and the blue USTC letters completely. Fill those areas with clean smooth matching beige facial skin, clean matching white muzzle, and clean white coat. The face should have NO eyes, eyebrows, glasses or mouth; leave the muzzle contour, two nose rings and cheek blush. This blank base will go BEHIND independently editable eyes, glasses, mouth and chest letters in a layered PSD. Do not draw closed eyes or any replacement facial feature. Do not add anything. Maintain EXACT locations and proportions of every unaffected element. True transparent background alpha.
